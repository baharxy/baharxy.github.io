About

I have a multidisciplinary background spanning network mathematics, security engineering, and applied research. My PhD focused on resource allocation and distributed optimisation for wireless networks, and I now lead security engineering work in industry — building backend systems, mitigating DNS abuse, and exploring AI security. I enjoy connecting rigorous theory with practical, production-grade systems.

Experience
